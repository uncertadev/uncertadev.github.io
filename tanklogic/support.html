TankLogic Support

Last updated: July 5, 2026

TankLogic helps modular tank manufacturers configure tanks, calculate volumes and bill of materials, and create professional quotes on mobile, tablet, and web.

01 Contact us

For questions, account access, billing, technical issues, or feedback, email us at uncertadev@gmail.com.

We aim to respond within two business days. Please include:
  • • Your organization name
  • • The email address on your TankLogic account
  • • A short description of the issue

02 What we can help with

  • • Signing in, password reset, and email verification
  • • Subscription plans, billing, and App Store purchases
  • • Team access, roles, and administrator setup
  • • Configurator, offers, orders, and PDF export questions
  • • Bug reports and feature requests

03 Before you write

⬇️
Confirm you are using the latest version of TankLogic from Google Play or the App Store.
📶
Check that your device has an active internet connection.
🏢
If your organization uses TankLogic, contact your company administrator for user permissions and account activation.
